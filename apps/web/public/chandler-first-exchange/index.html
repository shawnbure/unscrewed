What counts as progress

  1. A real person publishes a concrete offer and requested return.
  2. A different real person makes a specific proposal.
  3. Both people reply and clarify the exchange.
  4. Both agree to the scope, timing, and handoff.
  5. Both confirm completion after the exchange occurs.

Not ready to list yet? Start with the five-minute reuse check to decide whether borrowing, repairing, sharing, or trading fits the need. If a listing already exists, use the one-counterparty field note to make the next proposal answerable.
