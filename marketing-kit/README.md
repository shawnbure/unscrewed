# unscrewed marketing kit

Reusable assets for the UMass Amherst launch (and every campus after).

## Files
- **flyer.html** — US Letter flyer, "Don't dump it. Trade it." Move-out angle with a live QR to unscrewed.lol.
- **stickers.svg** — three sticker designs: round wordmark, "Don't dump it. Trade it." bar, and the "You're not crazy. You're awake." manifesto sticker.

## How to print

**Flyer → PDF:** open `flyer.html` in Chrome → Print → Destination "Save as PDF" → paper size Letter, margins **None**, "Background graphics" **on**. That's your print-ready PDF.
- The QR renders live from a public endpoint so it always points to unscrewed.lol. For a print shop / offline use, generate a QR PNG once (any free QR site, data = `https://unscrewed.lol`), drop it in this folder as `qr.png`, and change the flyer's `<img src>` to `qr.png`.

**Stickers:** send `stickers.svg` straight to a sticker printer (StickerMule, StickerApp, or a local shop) — SVG is vector, so it scales to any size cleanly. The round wordmark works great at 2–3 inches for laptops and water bottles.

## Why stickers matter
A sticker on a laptop is a free, permanent billboard that starts conversations in class and coffee shops. Cheapest viral object you can buy — order a big batch and hand them out at your first Trade Day.

## Reuse
For campus #2, only two things change: swap "UMass Amherst" in the flyer kicker, and regenerate the QR only if you use a campus-specific landing link. Everything else is the brand.
