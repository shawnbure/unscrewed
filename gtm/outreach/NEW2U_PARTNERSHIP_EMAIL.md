**Subject:** Free tool to cut UMass move-out waste even further

Hi [Name],

I saw New2U won the 2025 Campus Race to Zero Waste case-study title — congratulations, 100,000+ pounds diverted is a serious number.

I built something that might help push it higher. It's called **unscrewed** (unscrewed.lol) — a free platform where students trade goods and services directly with each other: furniture, mini-fridges, textbooks, even skills like tutoring or bike repair. No money, no fees, no corporation taking a cut. Think of it as the online matchmaking layer for the exact reuse behavior New2U already champions — students find each other *before* the item hits a dumpster.

The move-out window is where it could do the most good. When 13,000 students leave in the spring, a lot of usable stuff gets tossed simply because nobody nearby knew someone wanted it. unscrewed closes that gap in real time.

It's completely free for students and always will be — I'm a solo builder, not a company, and the whole point is to keep it that way. I'm not asking for money or a formal endorsement. I'd just love 15 minutes to show you how it works and explore whether it's worth mentioning to students during move-out — a line in a newsletter, a spot on the reuse page, or a table alongside a New2U collection event.

Would a short call sometime in the next couple of weeks work?

Thanks for the work you do — it's the same problem I'm trying to help solve.
